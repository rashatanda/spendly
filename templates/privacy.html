Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data: basic logs such as login timestamps, used solely to maintain service security.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data from your device beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to provide the Service to you:

  • To display your expense history and summaries.
  • To authenticate you when you sign in.
  • To respond to support requests you initiate.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running Spendly for you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers located in India. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and hashed password storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, your expense data and account information are permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. We may use minimal third-party infrastructure (such as hosting providers) to operate the Service. These providers process data only on our behalf and under confidentiality obligations.

We do not integrate with banks, payment processors, or any external financial services.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided on the site. We will respond within a reasonable time.
